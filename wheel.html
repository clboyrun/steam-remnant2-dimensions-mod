      Burn-down: when the work lands, 40 hours a week

      A projection, not a promise. One person works one ticket at a time. In-progress tickets go first, then tickets that are ready (To do, nothing unfinished blocking them), the one with the most work waiting behind it first. Each ticket is treated as closed when its estimated hours are used up, which makes the tickets behind it ready. Estimates are the only duration used. It covers open tickets only, whatever the wheel's “Include closed tickets” box says.
