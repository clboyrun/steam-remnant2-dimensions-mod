A Remnant 2 content mod

Dimensions

Four new worlds. Five new allies in Ward 13. A late game that keeps going.

Dimensions adds new worlds, campaigns, gear, NPCs and progression to Remnant 2, built as a single pak with no runtime scripting. Everything below is planned and in development.

01

New worlds

Each new world is an extra roll for one of the base campaign's slots. The base worlds stay in the pool. Each is also a harder story campaign you can pick once, and an Adventure option. They stay hidden until your character finishes a campaign, then appear on an outer ring of the world map.

Rolls in Yaesha's slot

World of Grimm

The Brothers Grimm the way they first wrote it down. Rotting half-timbered villages, mills, wells and towers in a deep German forest, full of bargains, curses and hunger.

  • 26 tales as dungeon ideas
  • Hansel and Gretel
  • Fitcher's Bird
  • The Juniper Tree
  • The Pied Piper

The Abandoned Arcade

A dead arcade with no enemies, only cabinets. Each portal is a working machine: put in a coin, grab the controls, and fade into its dungeon. Six machines roll per world; the rest are broken. Win three to reach the final boss, and win more to change that fight.

  • 9–12 cabinets in the pool
  • Maze chase
  • Side-scroller brawl
  • Collectables run

Win 3 of 6 to open the final boss

Any dungeon can roll Corrupted: every enemy inside spawns red and glitching. Up to a 10% chance on the hardest difficulties.

New gateways in the Labyrinth. After the full campaign, every new world can be reached from there.

03

Gear & upgrades

Upgrades meant to give the late game somewhere to go without breaking the base game's balance.

+30weapons

Masterwork weapons

Ten more levels for normal weapons (+21 to +30) and five for boss weapons (up to +15), on the base game's own damage curve.

20mutators

Gold mutators

Mutators climb to level 20, extending their own curves. Past level 10 their icons turn gold.

+10armor

Forged armor

Each Forge level adds 10% armor and +1 to every resistance.

8rings

Enchanted armor

Each enchanted armor piece holds one extra ring, shown right next to it on the character screen.

990pairs

Prism: every fusion

Every pair of Prism fragments gets a fusion, where the base game has only 23. At level 51, add a tenth fragment to merge a fifth fusion.

Sonar Neckpiece

An amulet that pulses every 10 seconds and finds everything within 25 yards: allies in green, hostiles in red.

The radar gun

A gun with a side screen that always shows the map, even while aiming. With the Neckpiece on, it shows what the sonar finds.

Corrupted Shop

Weapon corruption, a ring that brings out more corrupted enemies, a multi-crit ring, and a Shard Pouch that holds 50 shards.

04

Ascension

Max out an archetype, then take it further. The AI ascends it with a Simulacrum, you level it again, and the trait points it gives stay with you for good.

Handler

Your archetype at level 10, as in the base game.

Handler+

Level it again from 1. Trait gains become permanent, shown as blue-grey dots, and new perks open up.

Handler++

All ten points become permanent, and the archetype's remaining skills open for selection.

Max health

A dot for every mastery

A locked trait gains a point each time an archetype stage reaches level 10: up to 42 extra max health, each dot in the colour of the archetype that earned it.

Handler

New companions

More animals beyond the dog, such as a cat, a bird of prey or a bear.

05

Quality of life

Equipment sorting

Sort gear A–Z, Z–A, by upgrade level, or for weapons by highest or lowest DPS.

Quest Books on the map

A map tab of their own, so new quests don't crowd your list. A book vanishes once you own its item.

Completion tokens

A new inventory section that tracks which campaigns your character has finished.

A bigger world map

The new worlds sit on an outer ring around the base worlds, and only appear once you've earned them.
